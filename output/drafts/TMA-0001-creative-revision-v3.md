## Changes from v2

1. **Heater cause is explicit.** Scene 3 shows a blue-white spark under Mara's door;
   scene 4 shows the coil heater sparking behind her while Elias refuses to fix it.
   Narration: “Mara's heater had been sparking all week. She'd asked him to fix it.
   He told her to call the super.”
2. **Mara's warning is on the page.** The second obituary identifies Mara Kwan,
   apartment 4A, and states that she entered 4B to warn the occupant. Scene 8 is a
   macro of that subhead under Elias's thumb.
3. **Scene 9 is exactly two papers.** Elias lays his obituary and Mara's side by side
   on the floor. No dozens of editions, no unexplained stack.
4. **Narration is conversational.** The fragmented v2 opening was rebuilt into
   complete spoken sentences while keeping short beats at the reveal:
   “He was alive.” / “Same fire. Same hour. Different name.” / “His name. Mara's.”
5. **Runtime.** 129 words, 13 beats, 55.15 s estimate; 10 scenes, 54.65 s board.
6. **Opening and branding preserved.** The first image is still the obituary
   close-up; the low-opacity wordmark is present from the first frame, with no logo
   intro or end card.
